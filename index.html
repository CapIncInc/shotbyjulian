About

Julian,
behind the lens

I shoot whatever makes a place feel like itself: the chrome on an old hood, a scooter parked in the wrong light, the way a street goes quiet after rain.

Most of these frames come from walks, car meets and slow afternoons with a camera in hand. I edit warm and dark, keep the grain, and rarely crop the story out of a picture. If it made me stop, it goes in the archive.
